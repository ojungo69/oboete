# The AI for summaries: models chosen from each service's list, a simpler line, three steps to add one (decisions 78 to 80)

Decisions 78 to 80 (2026-11-07) make choosing the AI that writes the summaries, and its model, quicker to read and to do. They build on decision 69's settings page ([settings-redo.md](settings-redo.md)) and keep [spec-webui.md](spec-webui.md): every setting has a path in the page, both languages, a confirmation before what sends or removes data, keys written and never shown.

## What was found (2026-11-07, this PC's page at 1,280 px)

- **A model is changed two folds deep, by typing.** A line opens on its switch, arrows, calls a day, timeout and test; its model is a choice of one or two models written into the page (`AI_CATALOG`), else "Choose another model…" and typing. An entry with prices (Anthropic, Gemini) shows no choice at all: its model is fixed, since its prices are the model's, and changing it means opening "Entry settings", then "Edit the summarizer", and typing the model and its prices.
- **Two folds hold one entry's settings.** "Entry settings" (its removal, its saved and effective values, its URL, its key) and "Edit the summarizer" (name, URL, API kind, model, timeout, subscription, limits and prices), with the timeout in the line as well.
- **The OpenAI API is not offered.** It is added as "another OpenAI-compatible server", with its URL, model and prices typed. As oboete sends an OpenAI-kind request today, OpenAI's GPT-6 models would refuse it: they take no `temperature` unless their reasoning is `none`, which is not their default, and they bill a prompt written to their cache at 1.25 times the input price, which the spending ledger does not count (below).
- **Adding an AI** lists every service one under another, each already added one with a button to go to its line.

## What changes

1. **Decision 78: a model is chosen from the service's own list.** The page asks the viewer for an entry's models (`POST /api/providers/models`, below); the viewer calls the service's model list (`GET {base}/models`) with the entry's key and answers the model ids only. The choice lists them after the models the page knows, each marked "not checked": a service's list does not always say which models can write a summary's strict JSON, so the line says to run the connection test after choosing one. "Choose another model…" stays for typing. OpenRouter's free entry lists its `:free` models only. The Claude and Codex subscriptions run a local program, which has no list to call: their choices stay written in the page (Claude: haiku, sonnet, opus; Codex: gpt-6-luna, gpt-6.1-terra, gpt-6.1-sol, the models Codex 0.166.0 names). A list that cannot be read (the key refused, the service down) leaves the known models and the typing, with a line that says why; a line whose key is still to be saved asks for no list (a keyless server of the owner's is asked without one).
2. **Decision 78: an entry with prices changes model and prices together.** The page carries each paid service's prices for its models (below, from the services' price pages on 2026-11-07). Choosing one of them saves the entry's model and its prices as one edit of the entry's saved values; a listed model whose prices the page does not know is shown and cannot be chosen there, with a line that says its prices go in the entry's settings. A name with several entries changes each entry in its settings, as now.
3. **Decision 79: a line shows what is changed often.** Open, a line shows its model, its switch, its place (the arrows), its key (saved, or the field to paste one, or "Change the key"), the connection test and, when it rests, its resume. "Advanced settings", one fold, holds the rest: calls a day, timeout, URL, API kind, subscription, limits and prices, its saved and effective values, and its removal. "Entry settings" and "Edit the summarizer" go; an entry's editor is open inside "Advanced settings", so nothing in it is a fold within a fold. A name with several entries lists each entry there, as now.
4. **Decision 80: three steps to add an AI.** "Add an AI" shows the services as buttons (OpenAI, Anthropic, Gemini, Groq, OpenRouter, NVIDIA NIM, OpenCode Go, the Claude and Codex subscriptions, Other); one already added is marked "Added" and goes to its line. A chosen service asks for (1) its key, when it takes one, (2) its model, from the models the page knows with their prices, and (3) its place: first, after one of the AI in the list, or last (the default). "Add" creates the entry, off when it takes a key, then saves the key to it, which turns it on, then saves its place, each with the version the answer before gave; a step refused leaves the entry as far as it got, its line open on what is missing. The entry's full list of models is its line's once its key is saved.
5. **Decision 80: the OpenAI API.** A preset: `https://api.openai.com/v1`, the OpenAI API kind (Chat Completions), key file `OPENAI_API_KEY.md` named as the other presets' are, default model `gpt-6-luna` at $0.10 a million input tokens and $0.50 a million output tokens ($0.01 cached), its requests kept under 272,000 tokens, past which OpenAI doubles the input price. OpenAI does not train on API data unless the owner opts in, and keeps it 30 days for abuse monitoring (developers.openai.com, 2026-11-07). A request to OpenAI's own host is shaped as its models take it (below). On this PC it goes right after Anthropic, off until its key is saved: the owner adds it with the new steps.

## The models list

`POST /api/providers/models` takes `{version, selector, refresh}` as the connection test's preview takes its entry, the page's token checked as for every settings call. It answers `{models: [ids], from: "service" | "cache"}` or a refusal: `stale` (409, the page's version is not the file's), `unsupported` (422, a subscription's program), `no_key` (502, the entry's key file cannot be read or holds no key), `key_refused` (502, the service answered 401 or 403), `unavailable` (502, any other status, a redirect, a timeout, a body that is no list). The answer holds no key and no URL.

- **The call.** `GET {base_url}/models`, with the key as the entry's requests send it (`Authorization: Bearer`, or `x-api-key` and `anthropic-version` for the Messages API, `?limit=1000` there), the entry's own headers, 10 seconds, no redirect followed (a redirect would carry the key to another host), at most 8 MiB read.
- **The ids.** `data[].id`, Gemini's `models/` prefix taken off (Gemini's alone: another server's id is the model it answers); each id 1 to 200 characters of letters, digits and `._:/@+-`, else dropped; each once; at most 1,000. Kept by the service, as its host names it: OpenAI's `gpt-` and `o<digit>` models, not its audio, realtime, speech, transcription, image, search, embedding, moderation, `codex`, `-pro` or `instruct` ones (`-pro` and `codex` answer the Responses API only); Gemini's `gemini` models, not its embedding, image, speech, live or audio ones; Groq's active ones that write text (their `output_modalities`), not its Whisper, speech or guard ones; OpenRouter's, only `:free` ones for the free entry (`ModelRule::Free`); OpenCode Go's that answer `chat-completions` (a key of their `api`) and whose `capabilities` do not refuse `temperature`; NVIDIA's, not its embedding, rerank, reward, guard or safety ones; any other server's, all. A list's mark of structured output (OpenRouter's `structured_outputs`, Groq's `supported_features`, OpenCode Go's `structured_output`) is not asked for: Groq marks none on qwen3.8-27b, which has written oboete's summaries under its strict schema there (157 in the 30 days to 2026-11-07). So no list tells a model that cannot write them from one that can, and the page marks every listed model not checked.
- **The cache.** The viewer keeps a list ten minutes by what it depends on: the entry's name, URL, key (as `key_id`, never the key), API kind, whether it takes free models only, and its own headers (a project header can change the models a key sees); `refresh` reads it again, and lists past their ten minutes go as a new one comes. The page keeps a list by the entry's name, URL and API kind, reads it when the line opens, again once it is ten minutes old, and on "Read the list again", forgets every list after any provider write and any settings read again (a key saved may read a list refused before; a write may give a line another first entry of its name), and drops a list whose read began before that; a list that fails offers none of its models. Once a list is read, the models the page knows for that line are offered only where the list has them, its current model kept (80a): a service retires models, as OpenRouter did its free ones the page named. A list's draw waits while a write holds the page, and draws the page shown then.
- **What leaves the machine.** The key, to the entry's own URL, as every request of the entry sends it; no record, no prompt. No spend is counted: a list is free on every service.

## OpenAI's request (slice 80a)

OpenAI's GPT-6 models take Chat Completions with these rules ("Using GPT-6", the model pages and the Chat Completions reference on developers.openai.com, 2026-11-07):

- **Reasoning.** `reasoning_effort` is `none`, `low`, `medium` (the default), `high`, `xhigh` or `max`; GPT-6 Luna and GPT-6.1 Terra take `none`, GPT-6.1 Sol and GPT-6 Astra do not. When it is not `none`, `temperature`, `top_p` and `logprobs` are refused.
- **The answer's bound.** `max_completion_tokens` bounds the answer with its reasoning tokens; `max_tokens` is deprecated and not taken by OpenAI's reasoning models.
- **The prompt cache.** Caching is on by default: GPT-5.6 and later write the prompt to the cache at 1.25 times the input price (30 minutes) and read it at 0.1 times (0.05 on GPT-6.1 Sol and Terra). Implicitly the breakpoint is the end of the last user message, the whole prompt for oboete's one-message requests, which are each new: every call would pay the write and none the read. `prompt_cache_options.mode = "explicit"` with no breakpoint turns it off for GPT-5.6 and later. The usage gives `prompt_tokens_details.cache_write_tokens` and `cached_tokens`.

So a request to `api.openai.com` (its URL's host, in any case and whatever its port or path, as the page knows its prices) sends `max_completion_tokens` in place of `max_tokens`; for the models known to take `none` (gpt-6-luna, gpt-6.1-terra), `reasoning_effort: "none"` and the temperature oboete sends elsewhere; for its GPT-6 models, `prompt_cache_options: {"mode": "explicit"}`; for any other model no temperature and no reasoning effort, its own defaults. An entry's own `extra` still wins, as elsewhere. The usage's `cache_write_tokens` is billed at 1.25 times the input price and its cached tokens at a tenth, and where a call's usage is not known its input is priced at 1.25 times; another host's `cache_write_tokens` is not read, so its spend is counted as before. The connection test sends the same shape.
