# The settings page, folded as claude-mem's (decision 69)

Decision 69 (2026-11-02) makes the settings page as quick to read as claude-mem's settings, with nothing it can do taken away. This note records what was measured, what changes, and the order of the work. [spec-webui.md](spec-webui.md) still holds: every setting has a path in the page, both languages, a confirmation before a send or a removal, keys written and never shown, and the four no-cap switches of decision 64.

## What was measured (2026-11-02)

- **oboete, simple settings, 1280 px wide, this PC's store**: one page, 5,296 px tall, its eight sections all open: 21 inputs, 9 selects and 67 buttons.
  - The AI for summaries were ten entries drawn in full, about 2,290 px, whether in use or not.
  - The places never recorded were one framed row with its own button each, about 1,290 px.
- **claude-mem 15.8's settings** (read from `ui/viewer-bundle.js`, its worker too busy to draw the page that morning): one dialog, 1,200 px wide.
  - On its left, the context the next session receives.
  - On its right, four sections. Loading and Display open; Backups and Advanced closed.
  - Advanced holds the provider, and shows only the chosen provider's fields.

## What changes

1. **The groups fold.** The simple page is groups that fold, as the detailed page's are (`settingsGroup`). What agents are given and the AI for summaries open first; My Work written by AI, Search by meaning, Privacy (the places never recorded, masking, what was removed) and Agents start closed. A closed group's heading says what it holds now, so it need not be opened to be read: "At session start: on · With each prompt: off", "2 in use, tried in this order · $0.39 used this month", "3 places never recorded · Masking of secrets: on". A group stays as the owner left it through the redraws a poll or a save brings.
2. **One line for each AI.** The AI in use are numbered lines in the order they are tried: name, model, state. A line opens on what to change (its switch, its model, its key, a test, its place). One that needs the owner (a key to enter, a rest only the owner ends) opens by itself. The AI turned off fold together under "AI not in use (n)". The arrows move an AI past the ones turned off, to the next in use.
3. **The places never recorded fold** under their count; adding a place stays outside the fold.
4. **The month's spend in cents** ($0.39), under a cent as "<$0.01", where the page said $0.385626.
5. **One page, no Simple and Detailed switch** (69b-2a, built; see As built below). The detailed page's parts move into the groups they belong to, each group closing on its "More settings" fold, so the switch goes; each AI line opening its entry's editor in place (base URL, timeout, calls a day, limits) is 69b-3c. **Each change saves at once** (decision 69, 2026-11-02), as the simple page saves now and unlike claude-mem's one Save: a switch or a choice when it changes, a number or a one-line text when it is left or Enter is pressed, a text area (the summary language, a masking rule's pattern and keywords, the exceptions' hashes) when it is left, Enter adding a line there. Adding, editing or removing an AI entry keeps its own button, and what sends or removes data keeps its confirmation. The page's port moves the page, so it keeps a button of its own with a note of where the page goes.
6. **What the next session receives, behind a button** (a later slice): "See what is given" in the first group shows the Context view's text, where claude-mem shows it beside its settings.

## As built (69b-2a, 2026-11-02)

- **The page.** The lead ("Changes save at once. Actions that send or remove data ask first."), a config.toml error's recovery, the start guide (open on a new home), the warnings, then the six groups of 69a, then This computer and Maintenance, closed. This computer opens on a new home, and while the resident choice it shows is not saved.
- **More settings.** Each of the six groups ends on a fold of that name (`smallFold('more-<group>')`, closed at first and kept as left). Its detailed controls sit in a form of their own (`generalForm`), which carries the listeners of the saves made at once (69b-1); This computer is one too, holding the page's hidden save anchor. The guide, the simple controls, the preferences (a form of their own), the agents' setup and Maintenance stay outside every general form, so their events start no general save. The Agents group's More settings has no general form: its parts save through their own buttons.
- **No duplicates.** The detailed sections lose what the simple controls own: summaries on, My Work on and its estimate, the month's cap, its no cap and the month's spend, session start and each prompt, masking on or off, an AI's switch and its place (the chain table's order and switch columns), the search method (its detailed choice; the local model's files line moves to Search's More settings), the places never recorded, and the second list of what was removed (Maintenance keeps it). The providers section's Add button goes: the catalog's own server opens the new entry's form in More settings. The chain table keeps its model column, which clears a name's model, and calls a day and timeout until 69b-3c. An AI entry keeps its own switch and arrows (they act on the entry in the file, of which a name can have several) until 69b-3c.
- **Saves in turn.** The simple controls keep their partial saves and the detailed controls the whole-form save; both take one turn (`saveRun`). A simple save that finds a save on its way waits, its control disabled and the page inert, and builds its body from what is saved when its turn comes; a general change made during a simple save is saved after it. An arrow finds its AI by name in the order saved then and moves it past the next AI in use; an AI gone meanwhile moves nothing.
- **What outlives an answer.** A simple save's answer keeps the drafts of the general forms, the guide's phrase, and the simple page's confirmations open (masking off, no cap, a cap typed while no cap is saved) until what is saved matches them. A change waiting in the status line (a saved rule removed) is asked again after any answer.
- **Focus.** One rule for both kinds of control: a simple control by its simple focus name; any other by its field and its AI entry, with its caret, the folds around it opened. A field is drawn once, inside the general forms or outside them, so its field tells which: the page's tests count the controls so. An AI entry's key field in More settings has a focus name of its own, apart from its AI line's.
- **Links.** A link to a detailed control (an AI line's "More settings for …", search's Workers AI, an agent's connection settings, the repositories and name patterns) draws the page, opens the folds around the control and focuses it.
- **#803.** The key of what is saved takes the resident worker as saved, so a new home's recommended "on" counts as unsaved, and This computer stays open showing it. A simple save sends no resident choice and leaves it so. A general save sends the page as it shows it, so the next general change saves the resident choice shown with it; the guide's Apply saves it with what it applies, even when that changes nothing else.
- **Texts.** "More settings" ("細かい設定"); the sizes whose switches moved say what they size ("Size given at session start", "Size given with each prompt"); the texts only the detailed page used are gone.
- **Tests.** The page harness sends an event to the control's own form, and gives a list of selectors in document order, as a browser does. Hand mutants of the slice: 29, each failing a test.

## As built (69b-2b, 2026-11-03)

- **Measured** on this PC's store (raw.db 3.95 GB, 710,314 records; knowledge.db 3.81 GB), the resident viewer's reads, five each: the privacy state 685 ms (991 ms the first time), the settings 35 ms, the removed list 16 ms. Settings waited for all three before drawing. Of the privacy read, raw.db's distinct repositories take about 490 ms (no index holds `repo`, so the records are scanned whole) and knowledge's repositories with their counts about 200 ms. Every repository of raw.db's records was among knowledge's too (444 of 444): the raw scan adds only records the worker has not yet indexed.
- **Built.** Settings no longer waits for that read: it draws with the settings and the removed list, then reads the privacy state and draws it in. While it is read, and while it is read again after a save or a stale reload, the privacy blocks say they are being read ("Reading the repositories recorded…"), where they said it could not be read; a read that fails says so, as before.
- **Not built.** Making the read itself cheaper. An index on raw.db's `repo` would add to every hook's append, and its first build would scan raw.db whole while holding its writes; reading raw.db only past the full-text index's checkpoint would need each device's checkpoint. Neither is seen once the page does not wait for the read; they become worth it if the read grows.

## As built (69b-3a, 2026-11-03)

- **In place.** The privacy state, once read (on opening Settings, after a save, a stale reload, Refresh, an exclusion or a place never recorded), is drawn into its parts as search's state and the agents' are, without drawing the page again: the Privacy group's block, its heading's count, and in its More settings the repositories still recorded and the send exclusions (`renderPrivacy`). A write that holds the page while the read is on its way keeps its hold, and a field it refuses is marked on the page shown (issue #808). A field of a part drawn again keeps the focus by the focus rule, and a folder being typed its text, which the form holds. A place added clears the other blocks' saved receipts first, as the page drawn again did.
- **Refresh** clears the status line only when nothing was said on it since its click, so a confirmation asked meanwhile stays with its buttons.
- **Tests.** The page harness holds a provider's write and a simple change while the read is on its way and answers the read first; hand mutants of the slice: 10, each failing a test. Checked in a browser too: a simple change held while the read was on its way kept the page inert through the read, which filled the Privacy group in place.

## As built (69b-3b, 2026-11-03)

- **What a secret is for.** A key field (an AI line's, an AI entry's in More settings, the judge's) and the masking exceptions' exact value say what their value is for (`keepSecret`, as `data-secret`): a provider's key field by the field it is (an AI line's or its entry's in More settings) and the place, name, kind, API and address of the entry its save sends to (an entry whose address the viewer does not show keeps nothing, as it cannot be told from another); the judge's by its route, field and destination, whose addresses are fixed in the code, and Cloudflare's account typed beside it; the exact value by itself, as it is only hashed in the page. Search's Workers AI key is not kept: its endpoint may be set in config.toml beyond what the page shows.
- **Kept through a redraw.** The page drawn again (a save's answer, the language, a fold's link) reads the values being typed in the panel it replaces and puts each back into the field drawn for the same target, its button enabled as typing would, before the focus goes back (issue #807). The values are held only during that draw: never in `form`, the page's storage or a request other than the field's own save, which empties the field before it sends.
- **Not put back** into a field whose target changed: an entry moved, edited to another address or API, or another entry at its place; a judge destination chosen since, or another Cloudflare account typed; the other field of the same entry (its AI line's and its More settings'). A key is never offered to a field whose save would send it to another address or API (another entry of the same name, kind, API and address, at the place after a move, is the same destination); it is typed again there.
- **Tests.** The page harness types an AI line's key, an entry's and the exact value, lets the privacy read's answer come (drawn in place, 69b-3a), draws the page again by a save's answer and by the language, and looks for them in the form, the storage, the requests and the page's attributes; an entry moved, readdressed, given another API or replaced, two entries of one name whose address is not shown, and a judge destination changed, get nothing; the judge's key is kept, search's Workers AI key not; a key its own save sent is not put back after a stale reload or a refusal; a Cloudflare key is kept for its account and not once another is typed. Hand mutants of the slice: 12, each failing a test.
- **The keyboard's focus** (issue #810). Refresh of the privacy state, a send exclusion, and a place never recorded added or undone give the focus back once their part is drawn again in place, to the control that had it when they started (the button pressed, or the field whose Enter added a folder): the control of the same name, or, when its row left its list, the folder field (the Privacy group) or the pattern's button (More settings). A browser takes the focus off a control disabled or made inert while its read or write is on its way; the focus a keyboard moved elsewhere meanwhile stays there. The rows are named by their list, so a repository moving between the two lists takes no focus into the other. When the state is not read again, Refresh, still drawn, takes it. A part drawn in place gives the focus it had back by the focus rule (each of its controls has a name, the links to More settings included), or, on a fold's heading, to the heading of the same fold, so a focus moved there meanwhile is not taken back, however many rows the read changes. The page harness now moves the focus to the body off an element that leaves the page, as a browser does. Hand mutants: 15, each failing a test. Checked in a browser: Refresh, a folder added by Enter and undone from the keyboard each kept the keyboard's place.

## As built (69b-3c-1a, 2026-11-03)

- **The line's fields.** Each AI line opens on its switch and arrows, then its model, its calls a day with No cap, its timeout, its key, test and resume, and the link to More settings. Each field sets the name's `[chain]` value, so every entry of the name; a line of several entries says so ("These settings apply to all 2 entries of this name."), and values its entries differ in are said, not shown as one. The chain table in More settings is gone.
- **Model.** For an any or free rule, the list as before, with "The entry's own model" while `[chain]` sets one (saved as none) and "Another model…", which opens a text field saved to `[chain]` (a free rule's needs `:free`, as the server says). A `[chain]` model that not every entry takes is said so, and a fixed rule's line has a button to remove it.
- **Calls a day.** The field holds the `[chain]` value (empty when none), the value in effect as its placeholder. No cap (decision 64, no confirmation, its warning while on) sends `no_cap`; turned off with no number, the field opens for one, and left empty No cap is on again with nothing sent. A subscription says its calls a day ("Calls a day: 50", or that its entries differ) and has the switch only while an entry of its name is capped: the settings answer's chain row gains `capped` (#800 item 3).
- **Saved on leaving the field or Enter**, one change in `[chain]`, through the simple saves' turn, without holding the page: the field stays enabled, and Tab moves on (`hold: false`). What is typed meanwhile is a draft (`lineDrafts`) kept through every redraw; only a field's newest request sends, and only what is not saved then; Enter and the blur after it send once; a blur does not send again what a refused, failed or stale save sent. Entering Settings, a stale reload, a recovery and a name gone from the chain drop the drafts. After an answer, the row's edit is what a fresh load makes (`chainEditOf`), so the next general save sends what is saved.
- **A redraw is no leaving.** Chrome blurs the focused control a redraw takes away, the control still in the page then (checked in Chrome 156). A field saved when it is left (an AI line's, the month's cap, a folder never recorded) saves only when it is still on the page once the redraw is over (`onLeave`). The page harness's DOM blurs such a control as Chrome does. The focus rule keeps an AI line's caret too.
- **The judge's Cloudflare key** (issue #812) is named by the account typed beside it when the key is typed, not the one its field was drawn with: typed after its account, it is put back by the next redraw; typed for one account, it is not put back once another is typed, the one the field was drawn with included.
- **Tests.** The page harness: each field's trigger, its one change and the row's edit after the answer (a number, the entries' own, `no_cap`); invalid and unchanged; No cap on, off with a number, off with none, and back on empty, by Enter or by its switch; drafts through another save's answer and a language change; Enter then blur, and a blur after a stale reload, a refusal and a failure; values typed back before an answer and behind another save; drafts dropped; subscriptions capped or not; the model's other, its own and a fixed rule's removal. Hand mutants: 23 of the page and 1 of `capped`, each failing a test.
